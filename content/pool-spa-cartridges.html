Cartridges for the same filter housing are often sold under several part numbers from the equipment maker and from replacement-cartridge brands. When the label has worn off, owners usually have to identify the cartridge by measuring it.

What usually decides whether a cartridge fits

  • Length of the cartridge, end to end.
  • Outside diameter of the pleated media.
  • End caps: whether each end is open (a hole) or closed, the size of the hole, and any handle, thread or molded fitting.
  • Filter area (square feet of pleated media), which is printed by most makers and can differ between cartridges of the same outside size.
